About Franktek

Engineering comfort through innovation — the story and the standards behind it.

Franktek Engineering Services Nig. Ltd.

Franktek Engineering Services Nig. Ltd. is an engineering company focused on delivering innovative, reliable, and future-ready solutions that improve the way people and businesses experience their environments. With over 25 years of industry experience behind its expertise and two years of official operations, the company combines deep technical knowledge with modern engineering practices to create solutions that are efficient, functional, and built to last.

Operating across electrical services, smart-home systems integration, structural engineering, fabrication and metal works, and engineering consultancy, Franktek serves individuals and businesses seeking dependable engineering solutions that eliminate inefficiency, enhance comfort, and support long-term growth.

What sets Franktek apart is its commitment to innovation and the integration of modern technologies, particularly smart-home systems and advanced engineering solutions that bring greater convenience, control, and performance to everyday spaces. Rather than simply providing engineering services, the company aims to create environments that are safer, smarter, and more comfortable for the people who use them.
