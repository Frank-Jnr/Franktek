Our Services

Five core engineering disciplines, each handled to the same standard of care.

Electrical Services

From new installations to repairs and upgrades, our electrical work is carried out to a safe, code-compliant standard — for homes, offices, and commercial spaces alike. We handle everything from a single fault to a full rewire with the same attention to detail.

  • Residential and commercial wiring
  • Electrical panel installation and upgrades
  • Lighting design and installation
  • Fault-finding and repairs
  • Routine electrical maintenance

Smart-Home Systems Integration

We bring modern technology into everyday spaces — connecting power, security, and convenience into systems that are simple to live with. This is where Franktek's engineering expertise meets forward-looking technology, built on proven, currently available solutions.

  • Power control systems
  • Solar power systems
  • Smart home appliances
  • Automated gate systems
  • IoT (Internet of Things) devices

Structural Engineering

Sound structural design is the difference between a building that merely stands and one that's genuinely safe to live and work in. We provide structural design, analysis, and site supervision that keeps projects stable, compliant, and built to last.

  • Structural design and analysis
  • Site supervision and inspection
  • Load assessment
  • Renovation and reinforcement planning

Fabrication & Metal Works

Our fabrication work covers custom metalwork and structural steel, built to precise specification for both functional and architectural needs — from structural steel frameworks to bespoke metal fittings, engineered before it's built.

  • Structural steelwork
  • Custom metal fabrication
  • Gates, railings, and metal fittings
  • On-site welding and installation

Engineering Consultancy

Not every project starts with construction — some start with a question. Our consultancy service gives you independent, experienced technical guidance to plan, budget, and de-risk a project before a single wire is run or beam is set.

  • Project planning and feasibility
  • Technical assessments and site visits
  • Budgeting and specification guidance
  • Ongoing project oversight
