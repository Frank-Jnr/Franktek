Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer, and do you work with both individuals and businesses?

We offer electrical services, smart-home systems integration, structural engineering, fabrication and metal works, and engineering consultancy — and yes, we work with both individual homeowners and businesses.

Do you offer a free consultation or site visit before starting a project?

Yes, we offer a free consultation and site visit before any project begins, so we can properly understand your needs and scope the work accurately.

How long does a typical project take, from inquiry to completion?

Project timelines vary depending on the scale of the work, but on average, most projects are completed within a month or less.

What does your smart-home systems integration actually include?

Our smart-home systems integration covers a full range of standard offerings, including power control systems, solar power systems, smart home appliances, automated gate systems, and IoT (Internet of Things) devices — all built on proven, currently available technology to bring greater convenience, security, and efficiency to your space.

Do you offer any warranty or after-service support once a project is completed?

We provide after-service support for completed projects, though this isn't backed by an official written warranty.

How long has Franktek been in operation?

Franktek Engineering Services Nig. Ltd. completed a rebrand about two years ago, which is when the current name and identity became official. That said, the expertise behind the company goes back much further — our team draws on more than 25 years of hands-on engineering experience, which continues to shape the quality and reliability we bring to every project today.
